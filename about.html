A trading house built on repeat orders.

For decades, Global Enterprises has supplied building materials to contractors, developers and families finishing their own homes.

How we work

We started as a building materials trader and we still are one. That means our job is to find the right product for your site, price it honestly and get it to you on time.

Over the years our range has grown with what our clients asked for. It began with drainage and access covers, then grew into pool gratings, sports-surface channels and, more recently, kitchens and bathroom fittings.

Most of our business comes from people who have ordered before, or who were sent to us by someone who has. We'd like to keep it that way, so we don't sell a product we wouldn't install ourselves.

From enquiry to delivery

Most orders follow the same four steps.

  1. Send your requirement

    Message us on WhatsApp with sizes, quantities, drawings or a photo of the site.

  2. Get a clear quote

    We recommend the right product and send you a price for your job.

  3. Confirm and schedule

    Once you approve, we arrange stock and agree a delivery date.

  4. Delivery and support

    Materials arrive as quoted, and we stay on call for fitting questions.
